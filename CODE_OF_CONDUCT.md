# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in No BS AI to be a respectful experience for everyone who trains with the coach, deploys a copy, reports a bug, or sends a fix.

We pledge to make participation free of harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps this project:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the people using the coach
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, and any other space where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that breaks these standards by opening a GitHub issue, unless the report itself includes private personal information. In that case use GitHub private vulnerability reporting, described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and say that it is a conduct report.

The maintainers will review the report and take action they consider appropriate, including warning or removing a contribution. They will keep the reporter's private details private.
